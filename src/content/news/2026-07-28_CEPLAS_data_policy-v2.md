Our partners at CEPLAS have released a new version of their [research data policy](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21456568). This reworked policy strengthens the cooperation with DataPLANT for FAIR research data management.
CEPLAS already leverages [DataPLANT's ARChive](https://archive.nfdi4plants.org/communities/ceplas/) as the platform for FAIR data publications. Two years since their [first release](https://nfdi4plants.org/news/2024-05-06_ceplas_data_policy/), the updated version now incorporates the latest developments of user-interactive mechanisms for [FDO validation, revision and publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42979-026-05010-8) as well as other tools and services to boost data management, collaboration, and long-term reuse of FAIR data across the excellence cluster and facilitate its wide adoption.
