I am a data steward working at [IBG-4](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/ibg/ibg-4) at [Forschungszentrum Jülich (FZJ)](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en), 
supporting researchers in adopting the DataPLANT toolchain for FAIR research data management. 
My work focuses on helping users integrate DataPLANT tools into their workflows, providing guidance on best practices, and improving the overall user experience. 
I also maintain and develop the DataPLANT [broker ontology](https://github.com/nfdi4plants/nfdi4plants_ontology).
