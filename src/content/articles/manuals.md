DataPLANT offers several tools to facilitate RDM.
To help you make the most of these tools we offer detailed, step by step guides and examples in our knowledge base.
Here you can find guides for creating, managing ARCs, adding metadata annotation and sharing your data on the PLANTdataHUB with the ARC Commander, ARCitISA, or ARCmanager.

Explore our [knowledge base](https://nfdi4plants.org/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/) to find manuals for tools but also guides for e.g. contributing to ontologies and applying metadata standards.
